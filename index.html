How the week works

Every day follows the kids' rhythm. The main activity is in the morning while energy is high. Lunch is at 12:00. The car moves between areas during the 1–4 pm nap, then there's a lighter late-afternoon activity, an early dinner and bed around 19:30.

Daily rhythm

  • 07:30 breakfast
  • 09:00–11:45 main activity
  • 12:00 lunch
  • 13:00–14:30 💤 nap drive (1–1.5 h scenic route)
  • 15:00–17:30 light activity / pool
  • 17:30 early dinner · 19:30 bed

Where you sleep

  • Tue 1 – Wed 2 (2 nights): East · Seongsan / Pyoseon
  • Thu 3 – Sat 5 (3 nights): South · Jungmun resort area
  • Sun 6 – Mon 7 (2 nights): West · Aewol (25–35 min to the airport)

The route goes clockwise round the island, so no single drive is longer than the nap.

Before you go

🚐 Van & driving

  • Book a Kia Carnival 9-seater with 3 child seats. Korea requires car seats for children under 6, and the 6-year-old will probably still want a booster. Book early, since 9-seaters and child seats run out.
  • Bring an International Driving Permit, book type, valid for the whole rental, plus your Singapore licence, passport and a credit card in the driver's name. 9-seaters need Class B. 11-seat Staria-type vans can need Class D, so stick to 9 seats.
  • Rental companies often require drivers of large vans to be 26+ with 3 years' experience. Check before you book.
  • Use Naver Map or Kakao Map for directions. Google Maps doesn't give turn-by-turn driving in Korea.

🎟️ Book ahead

  • Scoot TR812 / TR813 with 7 seats together, and 20 kg+ bags (strollers usually go free).
  • Hotels: 2 nights East, 3 nights Jungmun, 2 nights Aewol. Ask each for early check-in, and pick ones with a pool or indoor pool if you can.
  • Online tickets are usually cheaper for Aqua Planet, Snoopy Garden, Eco Land, Hello Kitty Island, Camellia Hill and Hallim Park.
  • Check whether Singapore passports need a K-ETA for June 2027.

☀️ Early-June weather

  • Usually warm, about 20–25 °C. The rainy season normally starts mid to late June, so this week usually falls just before it, but pack light rain jackets.
  • Sun hats, kids' sunscreen, swimwear and a change of clothes in the van for beach stops. Bring sand toys from home.
